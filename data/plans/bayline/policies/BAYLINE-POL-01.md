# Initial Health Appointment

Policy No.: HS-IHA-01. Business Unit: Health Services. Product: Medi-Cal. Effective Date: 03/01/2026. Last Reviewed: 02/10/2026. Committee Approval: Clinical Quality Committee (CQC), 02/24/2026. Accountable Executive: Chief Medical Officer.

## Purpose

To set the standards Bayline Community Health (Bayline) and its Network Providers follow for the Initial Health Appointment (IHA), consistent with DHCS APL 26-001 and the Bayline Medi-Cal contract.

## Policy

Bayline will make sure that every Member, without exception for aid code or enrollment pathway, receives an IHA. New Members should have the IHA completed within one hundred twenty (120) days of enrollment.

The IHA is re-administered periodically according to the requirements of the DHCS Population Health Management (PHM) Policy Guide and the Bayline Medi-Cal contract. Health Services updates the re-administration schedule in the Provider Manual when DHCS revises the PHM Policy Guide.

The IHA must be performed by a Provider within the primary care medical setting, meaning a general practice, pediatrics, obstetrics, gynecology or internal medicine practice.

An IHA is not necessary if the Member's PCP determines that the Member's Medical Record contains complete information that was updated within the previous twenty-four (24) months. The PCP documents the determination in the Medical Record.

Network Providers deliver the IHA in accordance with BAYLINE-POL-03, Language Assistance and Culturally Responsive Care.

The IHA must be documented in the Member's Medical Record, including the date of the appointment, the rendering Provider and each required component.

For Members who also have Medicare coverage (Medi-Medi Members), Bayline applies the IHA requirements set out in the PHM Policy Guide and the Bayline Medi-Cal contract for dually eligible Members, and coordinates with the Member's Medicare plan to avoid duplicate appointments.

## Required Components

Each IHA must include:

- A history of the Member's physical and mental health.

- Identification of health risks, including clinical, behavioral and social drivers of health.

- An assessment of the need for preventive screenings and services.

- Patient education and anticipatory guidance tailored to the Member's needs, delivered by the Provider or a member of the care team during the appointment.

- The diagnosis and plan for treatment of any diseases found.

- An age-appropriate Staying Healthy Assessment (SHA) or Individual Health Education Behavioral Assessment (IHEBA). The SHA/IHEBA is a required component of every IHA and must be completed by the Member at the IHA and re-administered at the intervals on the SHA periodicity table.

## Procedure

Bayline's Enrollment unit sends the daily new-member file to Health Services and to each PCP's panel roster.

The Care Gap team calls each new Member within thirty (30) days of enrollment to help schedule the IHA, and mails an IHA reminder at day sixty (60) if no IHA encounter has been received.

PCPs submit IHA encounters with the codes listed in the Provider Manual. Health Services reconciles encounters against the new-member list monthly.

Health Services audits IHA completion and components through the Medical Record Review. Results under eighty percent (80%) for any element require a CAP.

## Definitions

Medi-Medi Member: A Member enrolled in both Medi-Cal and Medicare Part A and/or Part B, also called a dually eligible Member.

Primary Care Medical Setting: A practice designated as general practice, pediatrics, obstetrics, gynecology or internal medicine.
