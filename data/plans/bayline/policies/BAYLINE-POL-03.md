# Language Assistance and Culturally Responsive Care

Policy No.: HE-LA-03. Business Unit: Health Equity. Product: Medi-Cal. Effective Date: 01/15/2026. Last Reviewed: 01/06/2026. Committee Approval: Health Equity Council, 01/13/2026. Accountable Executive: Chief Health Equity Officer.

## Purpose

To make sure Bayline Community Health Members can communicate with their Providers and receive care, including the Initial Health Appointment (IHA), that respects their language, culture and health beliefs.

## Policy

Bayline offers free oral interpretation in any language and sign language interpretation for every Covered Service, including the IHA, at all hours.

Providers may not ask Members to bring their own interpreter, and may not rely on minors or family members except in an emergency or when the Member specifically requests it after being offered a free qualified interpreter.

PCPs must conduct the IHA in the Member's preferred language, through a bilingual clinician with verified language proficiency or a qualified interpreter, and must document the preferred language and interpreter use in the Medical Record.

Providers should adapt health education, risk discussions and treatment planning during the IHA to the Member's cultural background, health literacy and preferences.

Bayline translates IHA reminder letters, the Member Handbook and health education materials into its threshold languages: Spanish, Cantonese, Vietnamese and Russian. Alternative formats, including large print, audio and braille, are available on request.

## Procedure

Providers schedule interpreters through Bayline's Language Line partner by phone or the Provider Portal. Face-to-face interpreters are scheduled at least three (3) business days before the visit.

New Network Providers complete Bayline's Culturally Responsive Care training within ninety (90) days of contracting, and all PCP office staff complete a refresher every two (2) years.

The Health Equity team audits a sample of interpreter encounters each quarter for timeliness and Member satisfaction.

## Monitoring

Language access performance and grievances are reported to the Health Equity Council quarterly and to the Clinical Quality Committee annually.

## Definitions

Qualified Interpreter: An interpreter who has demonstrated proficiency in English and the other language, follows interpreter ethics and confidentiality standards, and can interpret medical terminology accurately.
