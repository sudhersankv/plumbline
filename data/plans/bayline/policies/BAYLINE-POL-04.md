# Regulatory Implementation and Policy Management

Policy No.: CO-REG-04. Business Unit: Compliance. Product: All lines. Effective Date: 01/01/2026. Last Reviewed: 12/09/2025. Committee Approval: Compliance Committee, 12/16/2025. Accountable Executive: Chief Compliance Officer.

## Purpose

To describe how Bayline Community Health implements new regulatory guidance, maintains its policies and procedures (P&Ps), and communicates requirements to its Subcontractors and Network Providers.

## Policy

Bayline maintains all contractually required P&Ps and reviews each P&P at least once a year.

The Compliance team logs each new DHCS All Plan Letter (APL) in the Compliance Tracker on the day it is released and assigns an accountable business owner.

## Implementation Procedure

The business owner reviews all P&Ps affected by the APL and records whether amendments are needed within twenty-one (21) days of release.

When amendments are needed, the business owner revises the P&Ps, obtains committee approval, and Compliance submits the updated P&Ps to the DHCS Managed Care Operations Division (MCOD)-MCP Submission Portal within ninety (90) calendar days of the APL release.

When no amendments are needed, Compliance uploads an attestation to the MCOD-MCP Submission Portal within ninety (90) calendar days of the APL release, stating that Bayline's P&Ps have been reviewed and no changes are necessary.

The subject line of each attestation shall contain the APL number and the APL title, for example "APL 26-001 Initial Health Appointment: P&P Attestation".

Compliance saves the Portal confirmation for each submission and attestation in the Compliance Tracker.

## Subcontractor and Network Provider Compliance

Bayline is responsible for making sure its Subcontractors and Network Providers comply with applicable state and federal laws and regulations, the DHCS contract and DHCS guidance, including APLs and Policy Letters.

Compliance sends each new APL to all Subcontractors within thirty (30) days of release, and Provider Relations communicates the APL to all Network Providers in the next Provider Bulletin as described in BAYLINE-POL-02.

Subcontractor and Network Provider Agreements are reviewed when a new APL is released to confirm they require compliance with the APL. Non-compliance may lead to a CAP or contract action.

## Monitoring

Compliance reports APL implementation status and Portal submission dates to the Compliance Committee monthly.

## Definitions

Subcontractor: An entity with a written agreement with Bayline to perform part of Bayline's obligations under the DHCS contract.

APL Distribution List: The list of Subcontractors maintained by Compliance for sending APLs and collecting acknowledgments.

## Appendix A: APL Distribution List Scope

For purposes of APL distribution, the APL Distribution List includes only Subcontractors holding an active delegation agreement for utilization management or credentialing. Other Subcontractors, including the telehealth vendor, the transportation broker and laboratory vendors, are not sent APLs and are not asked to acknowledge them.
