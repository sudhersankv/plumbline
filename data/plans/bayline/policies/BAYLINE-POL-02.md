# PCP and Network Provider Responsibilities

Policy No.: PR-NET-02. Business Unit: Provider Relations. Product: Medi-Cal. Effective Date: 04/01/2026. Last Reviewed: 03/12/2026. Committee Approval: Network Management Committee, 03/19/2026. Accountable Executive: Vice President, Provider Relations.

## Purpose

To describe the obligations of Bayline Community Health Primary Care Providers (PCPs) and Network Providers and the tools Bayline gives them to meet those obligations.

## Policy

Every Member is assigned a PCP. PCPs provide and coordinate primary care, complete Initial Health Appointments for their assigned Members under BAYLINE-POL-01, and maintain a Medical Record for each Member they treat.

PCPs must meet DHCS timely access standards, including non-urgent primary care appointments within ten (10) business days of request.

Network Providers agree in their Provider Agreement to comply with all applicable laws, the DHCS contract, and DHCS guidance including All Plan Letters, as communicated by Bayline.

## Provider Tools

Bayline gives PCPs access to the Bayline Care Gap Portal, which lists assigned Members who are due for an IHA, preventive services or chronic condition follow-up.

PCP offices participating in the Care Gap Portal may record IHA completion through the portal's IHA attestation screen. A completed portal attestation is accepted in lieu of documentation of the IHA in the Member's Medical Record and is sufficient for Medical Record Review purposes.

Bayline offers PCPs the Bayline IHA Visit Template, an EHR-ready template that prompts for each required IHA component.

## Network Communications

Provider Relations publishes the Bayline Provider Bulletin each month. Each bulletin lists new and revised APLs, a plain-language summary and the action Providers must take.

Provider Relations Representatives review new APL requirements with high-volume PCP offices during quarterly visits.

## Monitoring

Provider Relations reports Care Gap Portal adoption, IHA attestation volume and access survey results to the Network Management Committee quarterly.

## Definitions

Care Gap Portal: Bayline's secure web application that displays care gaps for a PCP's assigned Members and allows the office to attest to completion of selected services.
