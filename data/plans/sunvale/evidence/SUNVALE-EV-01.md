# Quality Improvement and Health Equity Committee Minutes, February 18, 2026

Meeting: Quality Improvement and Health Equity Committee (QIHEC), Regular Session. Date: February 18, 2026, 8:00 a.m. to 10:05 a.m. Location: Sunvale Health Plan, Conference Room 3B and video conference. Recorder: Tamsin Okafor, Quality Program Coordinator.

Voting members present: Dr. Rafael Ilunga, Chief Medical Officer (Chair); Dr. Priya Vaskar, Medical Director, Quality; Leonie Marchetti, Director, Quality Management; Dr. Samuel Ochoa-Reyes, network pediatrician; Dr. Helen Aberdeen, network internist; Marisol Tran, Chief Health Equity Officer. Quorum confirmed.

## 1. Call to Order and Approval of Minutes

The Chair called the meeting to order at 8:02 a.m. The minutes of the January 21, 2026 meeting were approved as written (motion: Dr. Aberdeen; second: Ms. Tran; unanimous).

## 2. Regulatory Update: DHCS APL 26-001, Initial Health Appointment

Ms. Marchetti presented the Compliance gap analysis for APL 26-001, released January 7, 2026, which supersedes APL 22-030. Compliance identified QM-IHA-101 (Initial Health Appointment), PNM-210 (Provider Network and PCP Responsibilities) and CMP-401 (P&P Management) as affected policies. CL-300 (Cultural and Linguistic Services) was reviewed and found to need no changes.

Key changes to QM-IHA-101 presented: terminology updated to "Initial Health Appointment"; explicit statement that the IHA is periodically re-administered at intervals in the PHM Policy Guide; 12-month PCP exception restated; Staying Healthy Assessment (SHA) and IHEBA requirement removed from the policy and the IHA Documentation Template.

Dr. Vaskar noted that the Medical Record Review tool will be updated so that SHA completion is no longer scored, effective with the Q2 2026 review cycle.

Dr. Ochoa-Reyes asked whether pediatric practices may continue to use the SHA voluntarily. Ms. Marchetti confirmed that providers may use any validated risk tool, but no specific instrument is required.

Motion to approve QM-IHA-101 as revised: Dr. Ochoa-Reyes; second: Dr. Aberdeen. Approved unanimously. Revised policy effective March 16, 2026.

Action item: Compliance to submit the approved P&Ps to the DHCS MCOD-MCP Submission Portal and to upload an attestation for CL-300. Owner: Compliance (Gideon Farr). Due: before April 7, 2026.

Action item: Provider Network Management to publish an APL 26-001 article in the March 2026 Provider Update bulletin. Owner: Provider Relations (Annika Bloom). Due: March 2026.

## 3. IHA Performance, Q4 2025

Ms. Marchetti reported the Q4 2025 IHA completion rate of 61.4% for new enrollees, below the 70% goal. Delegated medical groups performed at 57.9%; directly contracted PCPs at 66.0%.

The committee approved an outreach intervention adding text-message reminders at day 45 of enrollment, effective March 1, 2026.

## 4. Health Education and Cultural and Linguistic Services

Ms. Tran presented the 2025 Health Education and C&L Work Plan Evaluation. Interpreter fill rate was 97.8% and threshold-language translation turnaround met goal. The 2026 Work Plan was approved.

## 5. Adjournment

The meeting adjourned at 10:05 a.m. Next meeting: April 15, 2026.
