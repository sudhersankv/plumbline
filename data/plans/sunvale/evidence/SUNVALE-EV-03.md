# IHA Completion Report, Q4 2025 (October 1 to December 31, 2025)

Prepared by: Quality Management Analytics. Report date: February 6, 2026. Data source: enrollment data warehouse, encounter and claims data with 30-day run-out. Presented to QIHEC on February 18, 2026.

## Summary

This report measures completion of the Initial Health Assessment for Members newly enrolled between July 1 and September 30, 2025, whose 120-day window closed in Q4 2025.

Denominator: 7,986 newly enrolled Members with continuous enrollment through day 120.

Initial Health Assessment completed within 120 days: 4,903 Members (61.4%), below the 70% goal.

Members with a completed Staying Healthy Assessment at the visit: 3,544 of 4,903 (72.3%).

## Completion by Provider Type

Directly contracted PCPs: 2,710 of 4,106 (66.0%). Delegated medical groups: 2,193 of 3,880 (56.5%).

## Barriers Identified

Provider offices reported limited new-patient appointment availability in the Inland service area and a high no-show rate for IHA appointments scheduled more than 30 days out.

Outreach staff vacancies in November and December 2025 reduced the number of Members reached by telephone.

## Planned Interventions

Add text-message reminders at day 45 of enrollment beginning March 1, 2026.

Work with Provider Network Management to expand IHA appointment availability.

This report was the last completion report produced under APL 22-030 definitions. Reports from Q1 2026 onward use the APL 26-001 methodology.
