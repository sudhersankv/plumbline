# IHA Completion Report, Q2 2026 (April 1 to June 30, 2026)

Prepared by: Quality Management Analytics. Report date: August 4, 2026. Data source: enrollment data warehouse, encounter and claims data with 30-day run-out, delegate monthly IHA files. Presented to QIHEC on August 19, 2026.

## Summary

This report measures completion of the Initial Health Appointment (IHA) for Members newly enrolled in Sunvale Medi-Cal between January 1 and March 31, 2026, whose 120-day IHA window closed during Q2 2026.

Denominator: 8,412 newly enrolled Members with continuous enrollment through day 120. Members with Medicare Part A or Part B coverage (dually eligible Members) are excluded from the denominator because their IHA data are tracked by the Medicare Advantage team and are not included in this report.

IHA completed within 120 days of enrollment: 6,105 Members (72.6%), exceeding the 70% goal for the first time since Q3 2024. IHA completed after day 120 but by the report date: 381 Members (4.5%).

PCP determinations that an IHA was not necessary because the Medical Record was complete and updated within the prior 12 months: 214 Members (2.5%). These are counted as compliant.

Members with no IHA and no documented exception: 1,712 Members (20.4%). All 1,712 had at least three documented outreach attempts; see the Member Outreach Log for Q2 2026.

## Completion by Provider Type

Directly contracted PCPs: 3,288 of 4,390 (74.9%). Delegated medical groups: 2,817 of 4,022 (70.0%).

Coastal Physicians Medical Group: 1,402 of 1,951 (71.9%). Valle Verde IPA: 1,415 of 2,071 (68.3%).

## Completion by Rendering Site

Of the 6,105 IHAs completed within 120 days, 5,733 (93.9%) were rendered at a PCP office in general practice, family medicine, internal medicine, pediatrics or OB/GYN.

The remaining 372 IHAs (6.1%) were rendered at alternate sites: 241 by BrightPath Virtual Care telehealth clinicians and 131 at contracted urgent care centers, under the alternate-site provision in PNM-210. These were counted toward the 72.6% completion rate.

## Completion by Age Group

Members under 21: 2,954 of 3,866 (76.4%). Members 21 and over: 3,151 of 4,546 (69.3%).

## Re-administration Tracking

Starting in Q2 2026, Quality Management tracks periodic re-administration of the IHA for Members under 21 against the Bright Futures periodicity schedule. Of 11,208 continuously enrolled Members under 21 due for a periodic re-administration in Q2 2026, 7,622 (68.0%) had a qualifying visit.

Re-administration tracking for Members 21 and over has not yet been built; the data specification is scheduled for Q4 2026.
