# Focused Review: PCP Determinations That an IHA Was Not Necessary, Q2 2026

Prepared by: Quality Management, Clinical Quality Nurse Reviewer (Darnell Whitaker, RN). Report date: August 12, 2026.

## Background

APL 26-001 and QM-IHA-101 permit a PCP to determine that an IHA is not necessary when the Member's Medical Record contains complete information updated within the previous 12 months. In Q2 2026, 214 Members were reported as compliant on that basis.

The Sunvale Audit Protocol for IHA Exceptions (QM-AP-07) requires a random sample of at least 30 exception records each quarter, or all records if fewer than 30 exceptions are reported.

## Sample

Because of reviewer staffing shortages in July 2026, the review was limited to 3 records, selected from one directly contracted PCP office (Northgate Family Medicine).

## Findings

Record 1: Member seen for a comprehensive visit 7 months before enrollment. PCP determination documented and signed. Compliant.

Record 2: Member seen for a comprehensive visit 10 months before enrollment. PCP determination documented. Compliant.

Record 3: Member's most recent comprehensive visit 11 months before enrollment. PCP determination documented, but problem list not updated. Compliant with comment.

## Conclusion

All 3 sampled records met the 12-month standard and contained a documented PCP determination.

The full 30-record sample, including records from delegated medical groups, is deferred to the Q3 2026 review cycle.
