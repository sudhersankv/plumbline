# Provider Training Log, January to September 2026

Maintained by: Provider Relations, Provider Education Unit. Extract date: October 2, 2026. Source: Provider Education Learning Management System (LMS) and webinar attendance records.

## Session 1: APL 26-001 Initial Health Appointment Webinar (Directly Contracted PCPs)

Date delivered: March 24, 2026. Format: live webinar, recorded and posted to the Provider Portal. Presenter: Dr. Priya Vaskar, Medical Director, Quality.

Attendance: 412 attendees from 188 of 203 directly contracted PCP offices (92.6% of offices). Attendance roster retained in the LMS.

Content covered: IHA timeframes; who may perform the IHA; the 12-month PCP exception and how to document it; required IHA components; removal of the SHA/IHEBA requirement; encounter coding.

The risk identification segment included case examples on identifying clinical, behavioral and social risk factors (housing instability, food insecurity, tobacco and substance use) and documenting them in the IHA template. Post-test pass rate: 96%.
