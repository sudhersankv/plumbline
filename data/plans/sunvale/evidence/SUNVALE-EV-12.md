# Provider Update Bulletin, March 2026: Initial Health Appointment Changes (APL 26-001)

Publication: Sunvale Provider Update, Volume 14, Issue 3. Published: March 9, 2026. Distribution: posted to the Provider Portal; emailed to 2,640 Network Provider contacts, including all directly contracted PCP offices, specialists and ancillary providers, and the four delegated medical groups for forwarding to their Network Providers. Fax blast to 311 offices without email on file.

## What Changed

DHCS released All Plan Letter (APL) 26-001, Initial Health Appointment, on January 7, 2026. It replaces APL 22-030. Sunvale's revised IHA policy, QM-IHA-101, is effective March 16, 2026.

## What You Need to Do

Complete an IHA for every newly assigned Sunvale Medi-Cal Member within 120 days of enrollment, and re-administer it at the intervals in the DHCS PHM Policy Guide.

The IHA must be performed by a Provider in a primary care medical setting: general practice, family medicine, internal medicine, pediatrics, obstetrics or gynecology.

You do not need to repeat the IHA if you, as the Member's PCP, determine that the Medical Record contains complete information updated within the previous 12 months. Document your determination and the date of the qualifying visit in the chart.

Provide the IHA in the Member's preferred language. Free interpreters are available 24/7 through the Sunvale interpreter line.

Document the IHA in the Member's Medical Record.

## IHA Components

Every IHA must include a history of the Member's physical and mental health, identification of risks, an assessment of need for preventive screens or services, health education, and diagnosis and a treatment plan for any diseases.

The updated Sunvale IHA Documentation Template (form QM-F-22, rev. 03/2026) has separate fields for physical health history and for mental and behavioral health history, risk identification, preventive services, health education, and diagnosis and plan. Download it from the Provider Portal.

## Staying Healthy Assessment Retired

The Staying Healthy Assessment (SHA) and IHEBA are no longer required as part of the IHA. Sunvale will not score SHA completion in Medical Record Reviews beginning with the Q2 2026 cycle. You may still use a validated risk tool of your choice.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can a Member's IHA be completed at urgent care? A: See PNM-210 for alternate-site options when your office cannot offer a timely IHA appointment.

Q: Is health education still required if the visit is running long? A: Health education may be deferred to a later visit when time is limited. Refer the Member to Sunvale Health Education.
