# Medical Record Review Summary: IHA Elements, Q2 2026

Prepared by: Quality Management, Facility Site Review and Medical Record Review (FSR/MRR) Unit. Review period: records for IHAs rendered April 1 to June 30, 2026. Report date: July 28, 2026. Lead reviewer: Corinne Mathers, RN, Certified Site Reviewer.

## Methodology

The FSR/MRR Unit reviewed 30 Medical Records from 10 directly contracted PCP offices (3 records per office), selected at random from IHA encounters during the review period. Of the 30 records, 21 were adult Members and 9 were Members under 21.

The review used the Sunvale MRR Tool, version 2026.1, which was updated for APL 26-001. The SHA/IHEBA completion element was removed from the tool effective with this review cycle and is no longer scored.

Each element is scored as met or not met. The compliance threshold for each element is 80%.

## Results by Element

IHA documented in the Member's Medical Record, with date and rendering Provider: 30 of 30 (100%).

Physical health history (past medical history, medications, hospitalizations, allergies): 29 of 30 (96.7%).

Mental health history: not scored as a separate element in tool version 2026.1. Reviewers noted behavioral health history in some records, but this was not tallied.

Identification of risks (clinical, behavioral and social risk factors documented): 27 of 30 (90.0%).

Assessment of need for preventive screens or services (age-appropriate USPSTF or Bright Futures screenings assessed): 28 of 30 (93.3%). All 9 pediatric records documented developmental and lead screening assessment.

Health education provided and documented: 22 of 30 (73.3%), below the 80% threshold.

Diagnosis and plan for treatment of any identified diseases: 29 of 30 (96.7%).

Preferred language and interpreter use documented: 28 of 30 (93.3%).

## Corrective Actions

Health education element: CAPs issued to 4 PCP offices with 2 or more records missing health education. Three offices cited the Appendix A guidance in QM-IHA-101 that health education is optional when time is limited. CAP responses due September 30, 2026.

Mental health history: Quality Management will add a separate mental health history element in MRR Tool version 2026.2 for the Q4 2026 cycle.

## Conclusion

Overall IHA element compliance was 91.5% across scored elements. Results will be presented to QIHEC on August 19, 2026.
