# Interpreter Services Log Summary: IHA Appointments, Q2 2026

Prepared by: Health Equity Department, Language Access Program. Source: interpreter vendor (Lingua Bridge Language Services) encounter log matched to IHA encounter data. Report date: July 22, 2026.

## Overview

This summary covers interpreter services requested for appointments billed with IHA codes between April 1 and June 30, 2026.

IHA appointments with a Member preferred language other than English: 2,318. Interpreter requested: 2,206 (95.2%). Bilingual Provider with assessed proficiency conducted the visit: 97 (4.2%). Member declined interpreter, refusal documented: 15 (0.6%).

## Requests by Language

Spanish: 1,604. Vietnamese: 251. Tagalog: 138. Simplified Chinese (Mandarin and Cantonese): 129. Arabic: 41. American Sign Language: 18. Other languages (14 languages): 25.

## Modality and Timeliness

Video remote interpreting: 1,288. Telephone: 701. In-person: 217. In-person requests filled: 211 of 217 (97.2%). Average telephone connect time: 22 seconds.

## Quality Review

The Language Access Program reviewed 40 interpreted IHA encounters from the log. In 39 of 40 the Medical Record documented the Member's preferred language and the interpreter identification number.

One encounter at a delegated medical group listed the Member's adult son as interpreter without a documented offer of a qualified interpreter. The group was notified and the case referred to Delegation Oversight on July 15, 2026.

## Grievances

Language-access grievances related to IHA visits: 3, all resolved within 30 days. No grievances alleged denial of interpreter services.
