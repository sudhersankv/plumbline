# Member Outreach Log Summary: IHA Outreach, Q2 2026

Prepared by: Member Outreach Team, Member Services. Source: care management system outreach module. Report date: July 15, 2026. Supervisor: Lucia Fernández-Hale.

## Population

Members newly enrolled January 1 to March 31, 2026 with no IHA encounter by day 60 of enrollment, referred by Quality Management from the weekly IHA Eligibility Report: 3,871 Members.

## Outreach Attempts

Total outreach attempts logged: 13,094. Average attempts per Member: 3.4.

Telephone calls: 6,920 (reached Member: 2,233). Mailed IHA reminder letters in the Member's preferred threshold language: 3,871. Text-message reminders at day 45 and day 75: 2,303 (Members with a mobile number and text consent).

Members with at least three documented attempts, including one call and one letter, before day 120: 3,871 of 3,871 (100%).

## Outcomes

IHA scheduled as a result of outreach: 1,544. IHA completed by day 120 among outreach Members: 1,377. Members who declined an IHA, declination documented: 96. Unable to reach after three attempts: 1,712.

## Language

Letters sent in Spanish: 1,212; Vietnamese: 164; Tagalog: 98; Simplified Chinese: 87; English: 2,310. Outreach calls used bilingual staff or the interpreter line for 1,104 calls.

## Quality Check

The Outreach supervisor audited 50 randomly selected Member records. 49 of 50 had attempts logged with date, method and outcome; one record was missing the letter date and was corrected.
