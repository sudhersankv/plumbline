# Delegation Oversight Annual Audit Report: Coastal Physicians Medical Group, 2026

Audit type: Annual delegation audit, IHA and Quality Management functions. Auditor: Sunvale Delegation Oversight (Lead: Ivy Castellanos, Senior Delegation Auditor). Audit dates: June 8 to June 12, 2026. Report issued: July 1, 2026. Presented to Delegation Oversight Committee on July 8, 2026.

## Scope

The audit covered Coastal Physicians Medical Group (CPMG) delegated functions for the period January 1 to May 31, 2026, including IHA outreach, completion, documentation and reporting, using the Sunvale Delegation Audit Tool, IHA module version 2026.1.

The Medical Record Review sample included 30 IHA records from 12 CPMG primary care offices (internal medicine, family medicine and pediatrics), including 11 Members under 21.

## IHA Policies and Procedures

CPMG's IHA policy (CPMG-QM-14) was updated on March 2, 2026 to reflect APL 26-001. The policy requires the IHA to be performed by the Member's PCP or another Provider in a CPMG primary care office. Met.

## Medical Record Review Results

All 30 IHAs were rendered in CPMG primary care offices by a physician, nurse practitioner or physician assistant. Met.

IHA documented in the Medical Record: 30 of 30. Physical and mental health history: 28 of 30. Identification of risks: 27 of 30. Preventive screens assessed: 29 of 30, including developmental and lead screening assessment in all 11 pediatric records. Health education: 25 of 30. Diagnosis and treatment plan: 30 of 30.

## IHA Exception Determinations

The auditors reviewed CPMG's 30 most recent IHA exception closures. In 4 of 30, the care gap was closed by CPMG UM staff based on a comprehensive examination 13 to 22 months before enrollment, under the 24-month look-back in DO-510. No PCP determination was documented in those 4 records.

Finding: CPMG applied a 24-month look-back and allowed UM staff, rather than the PCP, to make the determination, which is not consistent with APL 26-001. CAP issued July 1, 2026.

## Corrective Action Plan

CAP CPMG-2026-03: CPMG to revise its exception workflow to require a PCP determination and a 12-month look-back. CAP response received July 29, 2026 and accepted. Implementation validation scheduled for the Q4 2026 focused audit; CAP remains open.

## Overall Result

CPMG scored 92% overall on the IHA module, above the 90% passing threshold, with one open CAP.
