# Initial Health Assessment Policy QM-IHA-101 (Superseded Version, 2023)

Policy Number: QM-IHA-101. Department: Quality Management. Revision Effective Date: 09/01/2023. Status: SUPERSEDED on 03/16/2026 by QM-IHA-101 (Initial Health Appointment). Retained in the policy archive for record-keeping.

## Purpose

To ensure that each new Sunvale Medi-Cal Member receives an Initial Health Assessment (IHA) in accordance with APL 22-030 and the DHCS contract.

## Policy

Sunvale shall ensure that an IHA is completed for each new Member within 120 days of enrollment.

The IHA shall be performed by the Member's PCP or another Provider in the primary care medical setting.

Each IHA shall include a history of the Member's physical and mental health, an identification of risks, an assessment of need for preventive screens or services, health education, and the diagnosis and plan for treatment of any diseases.

Each IHA shall also include an age-appropriate Staying Healthy Assessment (SHA) or Individual Health Education Behavioral Assessment (IHEBA), which the PCP must review with the Member and re-administer according to the SHA periodicity table.

The Medical Record Review shall score SHA completion as a required element.

## Communication to Providers and Delegates

Sunvale notified Network Providers and delegated medical groups of the SHA requirement in the October 2023 Provider Update and in the 2023 delegation agreements.
