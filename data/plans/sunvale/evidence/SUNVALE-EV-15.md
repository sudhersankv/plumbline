# Health Education and Cultural and Linguistic Services: 2026 Mid-Year Work Plan Report

Prepared by: Health Equity Department (Health Education and Language Access Programs). Reporting period: January 1 to June 30, 2026. Report date: August 5, 2026. Presented to QIHEC on August 19, 2026.

## Health Education Materials Supporting the IHA

The IHA Health Education Toolkit, revised in February 2026, contains 22 Member handouts covering nutrition, physical activity, tobacco cessation, immunizations, dental health, prenatal care, child development and safety, and behavioral health resources.

All 22 handouts are available in English and in Sunvale's four threshold languages (Spanish, Vietnamese, Tagalog and Simplified Chinese), at or below a sixth-grade reading level, and in large print on request. Translations were reviewed by qualified translators and a community reviewer for cultural appropriateness.

Toolkit orders by Network Providers, January to June 2026: 41,600 handouts shipped to 236 offices; 18,240 downloads from the Provider Portal.

## Health Education Referrals

Provider referrals to Sunvale Health Education from IHA visits: 1,127. Classes and coaching completed: diabetes prevention (212), tobacco cessation (148), prenatal education (301), asthma self-management (96).

## Language Access

Interpreter fill rate for all medical appointments: 98.1%. Threshold-language translation requests completed within 10 business days: 96.4%.

Member Experience Survey (Q1 2026), "Got an interpreter when needed": 91%, compared with 88% in 2025.

## Cultural Responsiveness

Health Equity partnered with three community-based organizations to hold focus groups with Vietnamese- and Tagalog-speaking Members on IHA barriers. Findings led to revised reminder letters that explain the IHA as a "welcome checkup" rather than an "assessment".

## Work Plan Status

Of 18 work plan activities, 14 are on track, 3 are delayed and 1 is not started. Delayed: cultural competency module for providers (moved to Q1 2027); health education materials in Arabic; community health worker pilot. Not started: Member advisory council session on IHA experience.
