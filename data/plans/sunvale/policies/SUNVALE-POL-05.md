# Delegation Oversight

Policy Number: DO-510. Department: Delegation Oversight. Line of Business: Medi-Cal. Original Effective Date: 05/01/2018. Revision Effective Date: 04/15/2026. Approved by: Delegation Oversight Committee, 04/08/2026. Policy Owner: Director, Delegation Oversight.

## Purpose

To describe how Sunvale Health Plan oversees Subcontractors and delegated entities that perform functions on its behalf, and how Sunvale ensures that these entities comply with the DHCS contract and DHCS guidance.

## Policy

Sunvale remains accountable for all functions it delegates. Every Subcontractor Agreement requires the Subcontractor to comply with all applicable state and federal laws and regulations, the DHCS contract and DHCS guidance, including All Plan Letters and Policy Letters.

Sunvale shall communicate new and revised APL requirements to all Subcontractors and their Downstream Subcontractors and Network Providers. Delegation Oversight sends each relevant APL with a summary of required actions within thirty (30) calendar days of release and tracks written acknowledgment from each Subcontractor.

Subcontractors that fail to acknowledge or implement an APL within the required timeframe are subject to a Corrective Action Plan (CAP), and Sunvale may impose financial penalties or revoke delegation as provided in the Subcontractor Agreement.

## Pre-Delegation and Annual Audits

Before delegating any function, Delegation Oversight conducts a pre-delegation audit of the prospective delegate's P&Ps, staffing and systems.

Each delegate is audited at least annually using the Sunvale Delegation Audit Tool. For delegated medical groups, the audit includes a Medical Record Review sample of at least thirty (30) records for IHA completion and IHA components.

Audit results, including CAPs, are presented to the Delegation Oversight Committee and summarized for the Compliance Committee.

## Delegated IHA Functions

Delegated medical groups are responsible for IHA outreach, scheduling and completion for their assigned Members and report IHA completion to Sunvale monthly.

Delegated medical groups shall follow SUNVALE-POL-01 for IHA content and documentation.

To reduce duplicate visits, a delegated medical group may close an IHA care gap without a new appointment when the Member's record contains a comprehensive examination completed within the prior twenty-four (24) months, as verified by the delegate's utilization management staff.

## Monitoring and Enforcement

Delegation Oversight monitors monthly delegate reports for timeliness and completeness and reviews quarterly performance against IHA goals.

Delegates with repeated deficiencies are escalated to the Delegation Oversight Committee, which may require a CAP, freeze new Member assignment or recommend termination of the delegation agreement.

## Definitions

Subcontractor: An individual or entity that has a Subcontractor Agreement with Sunvale that relates directly or indirectly to the performance of Sunvale's obligations under the DHCS contract, including delegated medical groups, the BrightPath Virtual Care telehealth vendor and the non-emergency medical transportation broker.

Delegated Entity: A Subcontractor to which Sunvale has delegated one or more of its contractual functions.
