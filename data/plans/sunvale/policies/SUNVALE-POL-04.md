# Policy and Procedure Management and Regulatory Implementation

Policy Number: CMP-401. Department: Compliance. Line of Business: All lines. Original Effective Date: 03/01/2017. Revision Effective Date: 01/15/2026. Approved by: Compliance Committee, 01/08/2026. Policy Owner: Chief Compliance Officer.

## Purpose

To establish how Sunvale Health Plan develops, reviews, approves and maintains its policies and procedures (P&Ps), and how Sunvale implements new or revised regulatory guidance, including DHCS All Plan Letters (APLs) and Policy Letters.

## Policy

All P&Ps are reviewed at least annually by the policy owner and approved by the appropriate oversight committee. Approved P&Ps are published in the Sunvale policy library.

The Compliance department monitors DHCS, DMHC and CMS websites and distribution lists daily for new or revised guidance and logs each item in the Regulatory Tracking System within two (2) business days of release.

## Regulatory Implementation Procedure

For each new or revised APL, the Regulatory Affairs Analyst assigns a business owner, identifies affected P&Ps and opens an implementation workplan in the Regulatory Tracking System.

The business owner completes a gap analysis comparing each affected P&P with the APL requirements and documents the result in the workplan within thirty (30) calendar days of the APL release.

If the APL requires a change to a contractually required P&P, the business owner drafts the revision and routes it for committee approval.

Sunvale submits updated P&Ps to the DHCS Managed Care Operations Division (MCOD)-MCP Submission Portal within one hundred twenty (120) calendar days of the release of the APL.

If the business owner determines that no changes to P&Ps are necessary, Compliance uploads an attestation to the MCOD-MCP Submission Portal stating that Sunvale's P&Ps have been reviewed and no changes are necessary.

Each attestation must include, in the subject line, the title of the APL and the APL release date (for example, "APL 26-001 Initial Health Appointment, released 01/07/2026: Attestation of No Change").

Compliance retains the Portal submission confirmation, the approved P&Ps and the attestation in the Regulatory Tracking System for at least ten (10) years.

## Communication of Requirements

Once an APL has been implemented, the business owner coordinates with Provider Network Management and Delegation Oversight to communicate the new requirements to Network Providers and Subcontractors, as described in SUNVALE-POL-02 and SUNVALE-POL-05.

## Monitoring

Compliance reports the status of every open APL workplan, including Portal submission dates, to the Compliance Committee each month.

Missed regulatory deadlines are reported to the Chief Compliance Officer and to the Board Compliance and Audit Committee at its next meeting.

## Definitions

Contractually Required P&P: A policy or procedure that the DHCS contract requires Sunvale to maintain and submit to DHCS.

MCOD-MCP Submission Portal: The DHCS portal used by Medi-Cal managed care plans to submit deliverables, including P&Ps and attestations.
