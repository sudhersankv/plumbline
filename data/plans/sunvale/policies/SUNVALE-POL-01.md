# Initial Health Appointment (IHA)

Policy Number: QM-IHA-101. Department: Quality Management. Line of Business: Medi-Cal. Original Effective Date: 07/01/2019. Revision Effective Date: 03/16/2026. Approved by: Quality Improvement and Health Equity Committee (QIHEC), 02/18/2026. Policy Owner: Director, Quality Management.

## Purpose

This policy describes how Sunvale Health Plan (Sunvale) ensures that each Member receives an Initial Health Appointment (IHA) in accordance with DHCS All Plan Letter (APL) 26-001, the Population Health Management (PHM) Policy Guide and the Sunvale Medi-Cal contract with the Department of Health Care Services (DHCS).

This revision replaces the term "Initial Health Assessment" with "Initial Health Appointment" throughout and removes language carried over from APL 22-030 and earlier guidance.

## Policy

Sunvale shall ensure that an IHA is completed for all Members. New Members are to receive an IHA within one hundred twenty (120) calendar days of enrollment with Sunvale, or within the timeframes in the PHM Policy Guide and the DHCS contract, whichever is more stringent.

After the initial appointment, the IHA is to be repeated at the intervals established in the DHCS PHM Policy Guide and Sunvale's contract with DHCS. The Quality Management department maintains the current re-administration schedule in the IHA Desktop Procedure and updates it whenever DHCS revises the PHM Policy Guide.

The IHA shall be performed by a Provider within the primary care medical setting. For purposes of this policy, the primary care medical setting means a general practice, pediatrics, obstetrics, gynecology or internal medicine practice, consistent with the definition of Primary Care Provider (PCP) in the DHCS contract.

An IHA is not necessary when the Member's assigned PCP determines that the Member's Medical Record contains complete information that was updated within the previous twelve (12) months. The PCP must document this determination, the date of the most recent qualifying visit and the basis for the determination in the Medical Record.

The IHA shall be provided in a manner that is culturally and linguistically appropriate for the Member, as described in SUNVALE-POL-03, Cultural and Linguistic Services, including the provision of interpreter services at no cost and the use of translated materials in the Member's preferred language.

The IHA, including any PCP determination that an IHA is not necessary, shall be documented in the Member's Medical Record.

## IHA Components

Each IHA shall include all of the following:

- A history of the Member's physical and mental health, including current medications, past illnesses and hospitalizations, behavioral health conditions and substance use history.

- An identification of the Member's risks, including clinical, behavioral and social risk factors relevant to the Member's age and health status.

- An assessment of the Member's need for preventive screens or services, using the current USPSTF A and B recommendations and Bright Futures periodicity schedule for Members under 21.

- Health education appropriate to the Member's identified needs, age and preferred language.

- The diagnosis and plan for treatment of any diseases identified during the appointment.

Sunvale does not require the Individual Health Education Behavioral Assessment (IHEBA) or the Staying Healthy Assessment (SHA) as a component of the IHA. Providers may continue to use any validated risk tool they choose to support risk identification, but no specific instrument is required by Sunvale.

## Procedure

Member Services sends each new Member a welcome packet within seven (7) calendar days of enrollment that explains the IHA and encourages the Member to schedule an appointment with the assigned PCP.

The Quality Management department runs the IHA Eligibility Report weekly from the enrollment data warehouse. Members without an IHA claim or encounter within sixty (60) days of enrollment are referred to the Member Outreach team.

Member Outreach makes at least three (3) documented outreach attempts, including at least one telephone call and one mailed letter, before the 120th day of enrollment. All attempts are logged in the outreach module of the care management system.

PCPs and their staff verify completion of the IHA through the Provider Portal Care Gap list and submit encounter data using the IHA CPT and HCPCS codes listed in the Provider Manual.

Quality Management reviews IHA completion through the annual Medical Record Review (MRR) and the Facility Site Review process. Findings below the eighty percent (80%) threshold for any IHA component result in a Corrective Action Plan (CAP) issued to the Provider.

IHA completion rates are reported quarterly to the QIHEC. The QIHEC reviews completion by county, by delegated entity and by age group and directs interventions where performance falls below goal.

## Definitions

Initial Health Appointment (IHA): An assessment completed during a Member's Encounter with a Provider within the primary care medical setting that assesses and manages the acute, chronic and preventive health needs of the Member.

Medical Record: The Member's clinical record maintained by the Provider, in paper or electronic form, consistent with Title 22 CCR and the DHCS contract.

Primary Care Provider (PCP): A Provider responsible for supervising, coordinating and providing initial and primary care to Members, practicing in general practice, internal medicine, pediatrics, obstetrics or gynecology.

## Appendix A: IHA Visit Guidance for Providers

The IHA should be scheduled as an extended visit of at least thirty (30) minutes where possible.

Providers should use the Sunvale IHA Documentation Template or an EHR template containing equivalent fields for each IHA component.

Health education is encouraged but is optional during the IHA when the appointment time is limited; in that case, the Provider may refer the Member to Sunvale's Health Education department or the Member's next routine visit in place of providing health education at the IHA.
