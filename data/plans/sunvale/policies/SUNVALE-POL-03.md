# Cultural and Linguistic Services

Policy Number: CL-300. Department: Health Equity. Line of Business: Medi-Cal. Original Effective Date: 01/01/2018. Revision Effective Date: 02/01/2026. Approved by: Quality Improvement and Health Equity Committee (QIHEC), 01/21/2026. Policy Owner: Chief Health Equity Officer.

## Purpose

To ensure that Sunvale Health Plan Members with Limited English Proficiency (LEP), Members who are deaf or hard of hearing, and Members from diverse cultural backgrounds receive Covered Services, including the Initial Health Appointment (IHA), in a culturally and linguistically appropriate manner.

## Policy

Sunvale provides interpreter services, including American Sign Language and other sign languages, at no cost to Members, twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week, for all medical points of contact, including the IHA and all other primary care visits.

Members shall not be required to use family members, friends or minors as interpreters. A Member may decline a qualified interpreter after being informed of the right to one at no cost; the refusal must be documented in the Medical Record.

Written Member information, including IHA reminder letters and health education materials, is translated into Sunvale's threshold languages: Spanish, Vietnamese, Tagalog and Simplified Chinese.

PCPs shall record each Member's preferred spoken and written language and any need for interpreter services in the Medical Record and shall conduct the IHA in the Member's preferred language using a qualified interpreter or a bilingual Provider whose language proficiency has been assessed.

Providers shall take into account the Member's cultural background, health beliefs and practices when completing the IHA, including when providing health education and developing the treatment plan.

## Procedure

Providers request telephone or video interpreters through the Sunvale interpreter line. In-person interpreters for scheduled visits, including IHAs, are requested at least five (5) business days in advance through the Provider Portal.

The Health Equity department reviews the interpreter services vendor log monthly for timeliness, fill rate and Member complaints.

Sunvale offers annual cultural competency and implicit bias training to Network Providers and their staff. New PCPs complete the training within ninety (90) days of joining the network.

Grievances related to language access or cultural sensitivity are routed to the Health Equity department for review within five (5) business days.

## Monitoring

The Health Equity department reports on interpreter utilization, threshold-language translation volume and language-access grievances to the QIHEC twice a year.

The Facility Site Review and Medical Record Review tools include elements confirming that the Member's preferred language and interpreter use are documented.

## Definitions

Qualified Interpreter: An individual who adheres to generally accepted interpreter ethics, has demonstrated proficiency in both English and the target language and can interpret effectively, accurately and impartially.

Threshold Language: A language identified by DHCS for a county based on the number of Medi-Cal beneficiaries who speak that language.
