# Provider Network and PCP Responsibilities

Policy Number: PNM-210. Department: Provider Network Management. Line of Business: Medi-Cal. Original Effective Date: 01/01/2020. Revision Effective Date: 04/01/2026. Approved by: Provider Network and Access Committee, 03/11/2026. Policy Owner: Vice President, Provider Network Management.

## Purpose

This policy sets out the responsibilities of Sunvale Health Plan Primary Care Providers (PCPs) and other Network Providers, and describes how Sunvale maintains an adequate network to deliver timely primary care to Medi-Cal Members.

## Policy

Sunvale assigns every Member to a PCP within ten (10) business days of enrollment. Members may select or change their PCP at any time by contacting Member Services.

PCPs are responsible for supervising, coordinating and providing initial and primary care to assigned Members, initiating referrals and maintaining continuity of care.

PCPs shall offer appointments in accordance with the DHCS timely access standards: urgent appointments within forty-eight (48) hours, non-urgent primary care appointments within ten (10) business days, and IHA appointments within the timeframes in SUNVALE-POL-01.

PCPs shall complete the Initial Health Appointment for newly assigned Members as described in SUNVALE-POL-01, and shall document each IHA in the Member's Medical Record.

## Network Provider Responsibilities

All Network Providers must comply with applicable state and federal law, the DHCS contract and DHCS guidance, including All Plan Letters and Policy Letters, as incorporated into the Network Provider Agreement and the Provider Manual.

Sunvale communicates new and revised APL requirements to all Network Providers through the monthly Provider Update bulletin, the Provider Manual and, where appropriate, fax blasts and Provider Portal notices. Each communication identifies the APL number, a summary of the requirement and the effective date.

PCPs must maintain Medical Records in accordance with Title 22 CCR and the Sunvale Medical Record Standards, and must make records available to Sunvale for Medical Record Review and Facility Site Review.

## Access Improvement and Alternate IHA Sites

Sunvale monitors PCP appointment availability through the annual Provider Appointment Availability Survey and secret shopper calls.

When a PCP office reports third-next-available appointment times longer than the IHA timeframe, the Provider Relations Representative works with the office on a scheduling plan, including extended hours and Saturday clinics.

To improve IHA timeliness, Sunvale permits IHAs to be completed by contracted urgent care centers and by Sunvale's contracted telehealth vendor, BrightPath Virtual Care, when the Member's assigned PCP has no IHA appointment available within the required timeframe. The urgent care center or BrightPath clinician completes the IHA and faxes a visit summary to the assigned PCP.

IHAs completed at alternate sites count toward the PCP's IHA completion rate for the Provider Incentive Program.

## Monitoring

Provider Network Management reports PCP-to-Member ratios, appointment availability and alternate-site IHA volume to the Provider Network and Access Committee each quarter.

PCPs who do not meet access standards for two (2) consecutive quarters are placed on a CAP. Continued non-compliance may result in closure of the PCP's panel to new Member assignment.

## Definitions

Network Provider: Any Provider or entity that has a Network Provider Agreement with Sunvale or a Sunvale Subcontractor and receives Medi-Cal funding to provide Covered Services.

Urgent Care Center: A contracted facility that provides walk-in care for conditions that require prompt attention but are not emergencies.
